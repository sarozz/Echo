## Full description

There's no cell tower at 4,500 metres. There's nothing on the wrong side of a mountain pass. There's no Wi-Fi when you're on the move. Echo doesn't need any of that.

Echo turns the phones in your group into a peer-to-peer Wi-Fi mesh. Messages, voice, and SOS travel directly between phones over short-range Wi-Fi — Google Nearby Connections on Android, MultipeerConnectivity on iOS — or hop between phones in your group to reach someone out of direct range. No servers, no internet, no SIM.

Built for:
• Trekkers on multi-day routes in the Annapurnas, Langtang, Everest region
• Motorcycle convoys through Mustang, Manang, and beyond
• Climbing teams at high camps
• Anyone outside a cell tower's reach who still wants their group to be reachable

What's in the box:
• Real-time text chat with a rugged, glove-friendly interface
• Push-to-talk voice (Trek mode) and hands-free voice activation (VOX, Ride mode)
• SOS broadcast — one button sends your location to the whole group with red-flag priority
• Group map showing every reachable peer (locations are end-to-end encrypted)
• Store-and-forward: messages reach peers who reconnect within 24 hours (72 hours for SOS)
• Works offline. Works in airplane mode. Works in Nepal where cell signal doesn't.

Privacy:
• End-to-end encrypted with AES-GCM-256, key derived from your group's join code via PBKDF2
• No telemetry, no analytics, no servers
• Echo never sends anything to the internet
• Your name and 2-character ID stay on your phone

Power:
• Designed for cold, gloved use — large tap targets, high-contrast dark interface
• Reduce-motion is respected; works the same with Voice Over and TalkBack
• English and Nepali interface (नेपाली)

Echo is in active development. Field reports welcome at the project page.
